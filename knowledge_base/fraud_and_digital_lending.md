# Fraud Prevention and Digital Lending Controls (Sample)

> Illustrative controls for a fictional bank, written for demonstration purposes.

## FRD-1 Document Tampering
Salary slips, bank statements and tax returns must be checked for signs of tampering, such as
inconsistent fonts, mismatched employer details or round-number salary credits. Suspected forged
documents must be reported to the fraud control unit and the application frozen.

## FRD-2 Velocity Checks
Multiple loan enquiries from the same applicant, phone number or device within a short period
indicate possible loan stacking. More than three bureau enquiries in thirty days requires
manual review of the applicant's total obligations.

## FRD-3 Synthetic Identity
Applications where the identity documents are valid but the credit history is very new and the
contact details are shared with other unrelated applicants must be treated as potential synthetic
identity fraud.

## DIG-1 Digital Consent
For applications made through mobile apps or partner platforms, the applicant's explicit consent
must be recorded before pulling the credit bureau report or accessing bank statement data.

## DIG-2 Lending Service Providers
When a partner platform sources the loan, the bank remains responsible for the credit decision.
Disbursement must go directly to the borrower's bank account and never through the partner's
account. The partner's name must be disclosed to the borrower.

## DIG-3 Cooling-off Period
Borrowers of digital loans may exit the loan within three days of disbursement by repaying the
principal and proportionate interest, without any penalty.
