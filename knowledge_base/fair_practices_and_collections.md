# Fair Practices, Pricing and Collections Code (Sample)

> Illustrative customer-protection policy for a fictional bank, written for demonstration purposes.

## FPC-1 Communicating Rejections
When an application is declined, the applicant must be informed in writing of the main reasons
for the decision within seven working days. Reasons must be stated in plain language and must not
reference internal model scores without explanation.

## FPC-2 Key Fact Statement
Before signing, every borrower must receive a key fact statement showing the annual percentage
rate, total interest payable, all fees and charges, and the repayment schedule.

## FPC-3 Risk-based Pricing
Interest rates may vary with the assessed risk grade, but the pricing grid must be approved by the
Asset Liability Committee and the same grid must be applied to all applicants in the same grade.
Pricing must never depend on protected characteristics.

## FPC-4 Prepayment
Borrowers of floating-rate retail loans may prepay in part or in full at any time without a
prepayment penalty.

## COLL-1 Early Delinquency Contact
When an EMI is missed, the borrower must be contacted within five days with a reminder and an
offer to discuss repayment difficulties. Recovery agents must not contact borrowers before 8 am or
after 7 pm, and must never use threats or harassment.

## COLL-2 Restructuring for Hardship
Borrowers facing temporary hardship such as job loss or medical emergency may request
restructuring. Options include a moratorium of up to six months or an extension of tenure. A
restructured account must be flagged and monitored for twelve months.

## COLL-3 Write-off and Recovery
An unsecured account that remains 180 days past due is classified as a loss asset and considered
for write-off. Write-off does not extinguish the borrower's liability, and recovery efforts continue.
