# Retail Lending Credit Policy (Sample)

> Illustrative internal policy for a fictional bank, written for demonstration purposes.
> It is not an official regulatory document.

## RLP-1 Scope
This policy applies to unsecured personal loans and secured retail loans (home and vehicle)
originated by the retail lending division. All applications must be assessed using the
approved probability-of-default (PD) model together with the manual checks in this policy.

## RLP-2 Debt-to-Income (DTI) Limits
The total monthly debt obligations of the applicant, including the proposed EMI, must not
exceed 50% of verified gross monthly income. Applications with DTI between 40% and 50%
require a documented mitigant (for example a co-applicant, collateral, or stable employment
of more than five years). Applications with DTI above 50% must be declined or escalated to the
Credit Committee.

## RLP-3 Credit Score Thresholds
Applicants with a bureau credit score below 600 are not eligible for unsecured loans.
Scores between 600 and 679 are classified as near-prime and require enhanced verification of
income and employment. Scores of 680 and above are prime.

## RLP-4 Delinquency History
Any account delinquent for 90 days or more in the last 24 months is a hard decline for
unsecured products. Two or more 30-day delinquencies in the last 12 months require
Credit Committee review.

## RLP-5 Loan-to-Value (LTV) for Secured Loans
For home loans the maximum LTV is 80%. For vehicle loans the maximum LTV is 85%.
LTV above these limits is not permitted regardless of credit score.

## RLP-6 Risk Grading and Decision Authority
The PD model output maps to risk grades as follows:
- Grade A: PD below 5% — branch-level approval.
- Grade B: PD from 5% to below 12% — regional credit manager approval.
- Grade C: PD from 12% to below 25% — Credit Committee approval with mitigants.
- Grade D: PD of 25% or above — decline unless an exception is approved by the Chief Risk Officer.

## RLP-7 Loan Amount Caps
Unsecured personal loans must not exceed 20 times verified monthly income, and in any case
must not exceed INR 25,00,000.

## RLP-8 Documentation of Decisions
Every credit decision must be recorded in a credit memo that states the PD, risk grade, key
risk drivers, policy checks performed, any exceptions, and the final recommendation. Credit
memos must be traceable to the data and model version used.
