# Collateral, Co-applicant and Guarantee Policy (Sample)

> Illustrative internal policy for a fictional bank, written for demonstration purposes.

## COL-1 Independent Valuation
Property offered as security must be valued by an empanelled independent valuer. For loans above
INR 50,00,000 two valuations are required and the lower value is used for the loan-to-value
calculation. Valuation reports older than six months must be refreshed before disbursement.

## COL-2 Title and Legal Verification
Clear and marketable title must be confirmed by the bank's legal panel before a home loan is
sanctioned. Properties with pending litigation, unclear inheritance or missing building approvals
are not acceptable as security.

## COL-3 Vehicle Hypothecation
For vehicle loans the bank's charge must be registered with the transport authority, and
comprehensive insurance naming the bank as loss payee must be maintained for the full tenure.

## COL-4 Co-applicants
A co-applicant's verified income may be added to the primary applicant's income when assessing
affordability, provided the co-applicant is an immediate family member and is jointly liable for
the loan. The co-applicant must complete the same KYC and income verification as the applicant.

## COL-5 Personal Guarantees
A third-party guarantee may be accepted as a mitigant for near-prime applicants. The guarantor
must have a credit score of at least 700 and a net worth of at least twice the loan amount.
Guarantees do not replace the requirement to meet the hard decline criteria.
