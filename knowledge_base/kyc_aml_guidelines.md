# KYC and AML Onboarding Guidelines (Sample)

> Illustrative onboarding guidelines for a fictional bank, written for demonstration purposes.

## KYC-1 Identity Verification
Every applicant must complete identity and address verification before a loan is sanctioned.
Applications with incomplete KYC must be placed on hold, not declined.

## KYC-2 Income Verification
Salaried applicants must provide salary slips for the last three months or bank statements for
six months. Self-employed applicants must provide income tax returns for the last two years.
Near-prime applicants (credit score 600–679) require both documents.

## KYC-3 Employment Stability
Applicants with less than one year in their current employment are considered higher risk and
the credit memo must note this as a risk factor.

## AML-1 Unusual Loan Purpose
Loan requests where the stated purpose is inconsistent with the applicant's profile, or where
the requested amount is unusually high relative to income, must be flagged for enhanced due
diligence.
